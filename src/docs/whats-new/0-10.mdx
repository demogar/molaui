## Approved with edits: a held tool call's third decision

A person often approves a tool call after changing one of its arguments: half the refund, the
rollout at 10% instead of 50%. Until now `ToolCall` could only be approved or denied, so an edited
call was recorded as approved, which claims the agent's call ran as proposed, and the record lost
what the person changed.

[Tool call](?path=/docs/ai-tool-call--overview) now has a third decision, `approved_with_edits`,
with its own glyph, word and ink. Pass `approval.edit` and the call offers **Edit and approve**
beside Approve and Deny, at the same weight:

```tsx
<ToolCall
  name="billing.issue_refund"
  status={decision ? 'running' : 'waiting'}
  args={refund}
  decision={decision}
  approval={{
    reason: 'It moves money back to the customer’s card.',
    onApprove: () => setDecision({ status: 'approved', by: 'M. Herrera' }),
    onDeny: () => setDecision({ status: 'denied', by: 'M. Herrera' }),
    edit: {
      render: (draft, setDraft) => <AmountField draft={draft} setDraft={setDraft} />,
      validate: (draft) => (draft.amount_usd > refund.amount_usd ? 'The refund can be at most the charge.' : null),
      onApprove: (edited) => setDecision({ status: 'approved_with_edits', by: 'M. Herrera', editedArgs: edited }),
    },
  }}
/>
```

- **The fields are yours.** Only the app knows which arguments a person may change and in what
  control, so `edit.render` draws them with your own `Field`s. Approve with edits is not offered
  until an argument has changed and `validate` accepts the draft; the reason is said beside it.
- **The decision is the record.** The new `decision` prop shows what was decided and who, under the
  header, so a collapsed call still says it, and for an edited call lists each changed argument
  before → after, as `FieldChanges` shows a field. The details keep the arguments as proposed and
  as approved. The decision is separate from `status`: one is about the person, the other about
  execution.
- **It is said as one sentence.** After deciding, focus moves to the decision: "Approved with edits
  by M. Herrera: amount_usd changed from 240 to 120."

Nothing changes for calls that do not pass `approval.edit` or `decision`.
