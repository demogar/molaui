# Iconography

Icons are [Lucide](https://lucide.dev), at a 1.5–2px stroke that matches the 1.5px keyline, sized
by the control they sit in (`size-3.5` small, `size-4` default). They are always decorative
(`aria-hidden`) next to a label, and an icon-only control must carry a `label`.

## Status marks are not icons

Run and step status use **square marks drawn from the cloth vocabulary** — hollow for queued,
working relleno for running, filled for done, crossed for failed — rather than the usual circles
and spinners. They are part of the system, not borrowed from an icon set, and they keep their
meaning in greyscale.
