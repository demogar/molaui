# Typography

Three voices, and the split is the point: the **system** speaks in Archivo, a **person or a
model** speaks to you in Alegreya, and the **machine's literals** are Martian Mono.

## The width axis

Archivo was chosen for its `wdth` axis, and it is used at three registers. Running text stays at
the drawn width (100). Display goes wide (118): a heading here is a shape cut to fill its panel,
and a bold grotesque set tight at normal width is the most common display register of the last
five years. Labels sit at 116 — at 11px, width is what makes caps read as lettering rather than a
tracked-out caption.

## Scale

The comfortable scale. The small end moves with density; the display end does not — a dense tool
is dense in its rows, not its page titles.

## Figures

Numbers compared down a column — costs, tokens, durations, ranks — are set with
`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` (the `tabular` utility) and right-aligned. That is the job a
monospace usually does in a dashboard, minus the costume.
